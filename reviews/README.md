# Reviews

Review records preserve the scope, gates, measurements and feedback of their dated checkpoints. A later cycle can supersede an old "next section" instruction or artifact-size measurement without rewriting that history. Use [Progress](../PROGRESS.md) and the [current handoff](../docs/SESSION_HANDOFF.md) for the live project state and next permitted action.

- `stage-3-calibration/` — feedback on the first complete section.
- [`stage-4-section-reviews/`](stage-4-section-reviews/) — section-by-section review records, including the [approved 1.6 review](stage-4-section-reviews/01-06-collections-symbols-and-iteration.md).
- `stage-5-final-audit/` — final research refresh and coverage audit.
